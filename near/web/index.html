Hold. Show up. Loot.

Every day the band raids. Holders check in with tokens held for at least a day. If enough of the band shows up, the raiders are minted a share and pull loot from the chest. If it does not, part of the chest is bought back and burned. Lots still level up the longer you hold, and the level still weighs your DAO vote. Everything runs in the contract: no admin method, no pause, no parameter setter. The deployer's last keys on this deployment are deleted on 13 Nov 2026; after that, nobody can change the code either.

A raid is won when the raiders' seasoned balance reaches 25% of the supply and at least 10 accounts join. Each raider is minted 0.1% of what it joined with times the streak bonus (0.11% on the first win, up to 0.2% on a 10-raid streak), and the raid's 20 loot pulls are shared by joined balance, so splitting a bag across accounts earns nothing extra.
