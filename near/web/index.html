Under the hood

Every day the band raids. Holders check in with tokens held for at least a day. If at least 10 raiders join with 25% of the supply, the raiders are minted a share and pull loot from the chest. If not, part of the chest is bought back and burned. Each lot of tokens gains a level per day held (0 to 10), and the vote in the DAO is tokens × level. Everything runs in the contract: no admin method, no pause, no parameter setter. The deployer's last keys on this deployment are deleted on 13 Nov 2026; after that, nobody can change the code either.

Raid numbers and exact rules

A raid is won when the raiders' seasoned balance reaches 25% of the supply and at least 10 accounts join. Each raider is minted 0.1% of what it joined with times the streak bonus (0.11% on the first win, up to 0.2% on a 10-raid streak), and the raid's 20 loot pulls are shared by joined balance, so splitting a bag across accounts earns nothing extra.

Under the hood

The buyback opens when the price falls 20% under the recent high (today and yesterday). Anyone, or the keeper agent, can call it; each call spends 10% of the reserve, at most 20% per day, and burns what it buys.
