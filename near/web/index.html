    A raid is won when the raiders' seasoned balance reaches 25% of the supply and at least 10 accounts join. Each raider is minted 0.1% of what it joined with times the streak bonus (0.11% on the first win, up to 0.2% on a 10-raid streak), and the raid's 20 loot pulls are shared by joined balance, so splitting a bag across accounts earns nothing extra. Lost raid: 10% of the chest buys BAND back and burns it. Every parameter is set at launch and readable with config; the contract has no admin method, no pause, no setter, and the deployer's last keys on this deployment are deleted on 13 Nov 2026.

    NEP-171 tokens minted by the contract. nft_transfer always fails. Each badge adds two levels to the loot odds, up to double the base odds for epic and legendary pulls.

    // 05 market

    Buy and sell BAND

    Built into the contract. If the price drops hard, a reserve buys tokens back and burns them.

    Loading testnet state…

    Under the hood

    The buyback opens when the price falls 20% under the recent high (today and yesterday). Anyone, or the keeper agent, can call it; each call spends 10% of the reserve, at most 20% per day, and burns what it buys.
