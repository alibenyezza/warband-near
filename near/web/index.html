Hold longer, weigh more.

Every lot of BAND gains a level for each day it is held, up to 10. Your vote in the war chest DAO is Σ lot × level, so a fresh whale weighs nothing and a patient holder decides. Selling only resets what you sell. The rules are in the contract; nobody holds its keys.
